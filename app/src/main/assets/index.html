Sortable song library, audio/video seeking, adjustable lyric sync, shuffle/reorder queue, built-in MIDI synth, MP3/MP4 audio, timed MIDI/KAR and LRC lyrics. Standard MIDI format 0/1 supported. No accounts, ads, tracking, or cloud uploads. Pair only phones you trust on a private network. Restart the app to revoke remote sessions.
